Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to rent a car?

A valid ID card or passport and a valid driving license. A deposit may be required depending on the car.

What is the minimum age to rent?

Drivers must be at least 21 years old with a license held for at least one year. Some luxury cars require 25+.

Is insurance included?

Yes. All our cars are licensed and insured. Ask us about extra coverage options.

How do I book a car?

Fill in the booking form on this page or message us on WhatsApp. We confirm availability within minutes, then you pick up the car from our office in Ein Misbah.

Do you offer weekly or monthly rentals?

Yes. Longer rentals get special prices. Message us on WhatsApp for an offer.

How do I pay?

Cash or bank transfer when you pick up the car. Contact us for other payment methods.
